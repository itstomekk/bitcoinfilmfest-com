## About the film

*Immutable History, Chapter 1* is a short documentary by Javier Borrayo, made with Simple Proof, a company that uses Bitcoin to prove that digital records existed at a given moment and were not altered afterwards. The film is about Screven County, Georgia, whose officials timestamped their 2024 election reports on the Bitcoin blockchain. Simple Proof lists the film under 2024, the year of the election it covers. It was published online in early March 2025 and runs just under nine minutes.

## The Immutable films

Javier Borrayo has made two films in this line for Simple Proof, both about using Bitcoin to protect public records.

- **[Immutable Democracy](/cinema/films/immutable-democracy/)** (2023, 17 min). The earlier film follows the immutable backup of Guatemala's 2023 presidential election. It premiered at the Plan B Lugano conference on 22 October 2023 and screened at Bitcoin FilmFest Mini in San Salvador on 7 November 2023.
- **Immutable History, Chapter 1** (2025, 9 min). This page. The same idea applied to a United States county.

Chapter 1 suggests more chapters to come. For any later chapter, see Simple Proof's film page.
