## About the film

The full title is Coined in Watercolor: The Art of Going All In. Gavin Robertson and TJ Murphy were neighbours in Seattle. Their conversations about Bitcoin, art and the rock opera TJ was writing led Gavin to propose a documentary about TJ's journey into painting. It is Gavin's debut documentary. They filmed in Seattle, then travelled together to a Bitcoin conference in Gavin's home town, Cape Town, where TJ showed his paintings.

## At Bitcoin FilmFest

The film premiered on Friday, June 5, 2026 at Kinoteka in Warsaw, in the afternoon block "Can everyone be an artist now?". TJ painted the festival poster for the premiere from a frame of the film, showing himself meditating on Table Mountain, and showed two more paintings made for the festival outside the cinema entrance. It runs about 12 minutes and is free to watch on YouTube.
