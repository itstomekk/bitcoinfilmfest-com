## Overview

*The Maxis Club Show* is an animated Bitcoin sitcom by Redy, the artist behind Maxis Club, a daily Bitcoin meme collection that started in April 2022 and aims for 2,100 memes. The series stars Maxi, a frog inspired by Pepe, and his friends. It was crowdfunded on Geyser and billed as the first animated Bitcoin sitcom.

## Chapters

- **Chapter 1** came out on 21 June 2024.
- **Chapter 2, Christmas Special** followed in December 2025.
- **Chapter 3, Fiat-Backed Things** premiered on YouTube on 4 May 2026 (about 23 minutes). A fiat inflation monster attacks the town, and the gang learns that Bitcoin is the natural predator of fiat.

The Executive Producer is credited on the official site as Tselly (@Don_Tsell), with Chicken Tender Productions.

## Bitcoin FilmFest connection

Chapter 3 screened at Bitcoin FilmFest 2026 in Warsaw and won the Golden Rabbit for Best Short at the gala on 7 June 2026. The series has also been shown at BTC Prague.
