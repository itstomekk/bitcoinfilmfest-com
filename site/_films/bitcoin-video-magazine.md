## Format

Each episode is an Issue of about 60 minutes by the official site's spec; on YouTube Issue #1 runs 62 minutes and the Issue #2 re-edit 78 minutes. An Issue is divided into chapters of roughly 3 to 7 minutes. The official site lists the recurring chapters as the newsdesk, podcast highlights, lifestyle and culture, Pleb Profile, Tech Deck, mining and energy, company profile and community, followed by credits.

## Issues

- **Issue #1, "The Genesis Issue"** premiered on YouTube on January 3, 2026.
- **Issue #2, "Future Primitive"** is listed on the official site as the latest issue. The YouTube upload is labelled a re-imagined re-edit and is dated May 16, 2026; an earlier release date for the first cut was not found.
- **Issue #3** was announced as coming soon in a YouTube Short on August 20, 2026. No release is listed yet.

## The Bitcoin Motorcycle Diaries

"A Day in the Life: The Bitcoin Motorcycle Diaries" is a Pleb Profile chapter in Issue #2, with Thomas Grobatt. The Bitcoin FilmFest 2026 programme listed Bitcoin Motorcycle Diaries from Bitcoin Video Magazine and described a short on-stage chapter at the festival. According to the channel, the festival segment, uploaded to YouTube on June 8, 2026 (about 10 minutes), pairs an intro made only for Bitcoin FilmFest 2026 with that chapter.

## Team, as credited on the official site

Tim Tayshun is credited as founder and executive producer. 4th Wall Digital is credited as producer and senior editor, Bitcoin Bazooka as lead editor and motion creative advisor, and genXbtc as full-stack development and tech lead.
