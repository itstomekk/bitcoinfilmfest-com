## About the project

*The Anatomy of Bitcoin* is an open-source, community-funded film that aims to give viewers a visual understanding of how Bitcoin works. It is led by Pedro, who is credited on the High Hash Rate podcast as its founder, and developed in public: the repository is licensed under MIT and anyone can contribute time, talent or bitcoin.

The film is still being made. So far the team has released short clips such as *Genesis Block*, *The Chain*, *Sovereign Walk*, *Elliptic Curve* and *Node Handshake*, along with research projects including an interactive 3D Bitcoin explorer.

## At the festival

The film was on the programme of Bitcoin FilmFest: Plan B in Lugano on 22 October 2023.
