synopsis: >
  The film chronicles the origins of BTCPay Server and the role of Nicolas
  Dorier. In 2017 BitPay backed SegWit2x. Dorier reacted by building a free,
  open-source payment processor that lets merchants accept Bitcoin directly.
  The story is told through BTCPay contributor interviews and recordings from
  2015 to 2019.
bitcoin_angle: >
  Bitcoin is the whole subject. The film covers the block size wars, the 2017
  SegWit2x dispute and the case for verifying your own transactions instead of
  relying on a trusted third party. It also follows how BTCPay Server helped
  merchants and Bitcoin conferences accept payments directly. Filming began at
  the Bitcoin 2023 conference in Miami.
