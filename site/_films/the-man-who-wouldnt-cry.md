synopsis: >
  Martin, an illustrator at an advertising agency, refuses to show grief when a
  beloved actress dies. His boss orders him to cry and shuts him in a room with
  a colleague whose job is to make him start. The Swedish original title is
  Mannen som inte ville gråta.
bitcoin_angle: >
  This is not a film about Bitcoin. Bitcoin FilmFest programmed it in the 2025
  selection. The story is about conformity and independent thinking, adapted
  from Stig Dagerman's 1947 short story. The filmmakers say they connected that
  theme to group behavior on social media and to freedom of expression and
  individual rights.
